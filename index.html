Luis Mota

AI Trust & Safety Analyst — Builder of Maldros

I spent two years adjudicating and analyzing flagged content at Roblox — threats, fraud, self-harm, account takeover, coercion, scams, and more — each case testing where policy assumptions broke down in practice.

I then advised industrial distribution companies across Latin America on strategy and analytics for two years.

Solving problems across different industries and independent work allowed me to develop a simple framework for reasoning through very complex problems: abstract a problem down to what's actually true, isolate the constraint genuinely limiting it, and solve that first with the head of department or principal engineers, not the symptom beside it.

I've since brought that process back to trust & safety as a builder rather than a reviewer. Independent research now runs alongside that work — into the regulatory and operational constraints T&S teams actually face: review-workflow throughput, the user-protection tradeoff, and what it takes to govern a production AI system.

That research feeds directly into Maldros, a multi-agent platform I designed and built to investigate fraud, abuse, and safety-bypass patterns at a scale no human reviewer can match. Its governance isn't a feature added on top — it's the system. Deterministic checks can veto the model's own conclusions. Every finding has to survive adversarial red-teaming. No report ships without meeting citation requirements. What I once enforced through personal discipline as a reviewer, Maldros now enforces by design.
