Luis Mota

AI Trust & Safety Analyst — Builder of Maldros

I spent two years making judgment calls on flagged content at Roblox — threats, fraud, self-harm — where policy definitions meet cases the policy didn't quite anticipate.

I then advised industrial companies across Latin America on strategy and analytics for two years — a different domain, not a different kind of problem.

Solving problems across those arenas converged on the same analytical process, one I still rely on: abstract a problem down to what's actually true, isolate the constraint genuinely limiting it, and solve that first, not the symptom beside it.

I've since brought that process back to trust & safety as a builder rather than a reviewer. Independent research into the regulatory and operational constraints T&S teams actually face — review-workflow throughput, the user-protection tradeoff, what governing a production AI system requires — runs alongside Maldros, a multi-agent platform I designed and built to investigate fraud, abuse, and safety-bypass patterns at a scale a human reviewer can't match. The governance is not incidental to the system; it is the system: deterministic checks with veto authority over the model's own conclusions, adversarial red-teaming a finding must survive before it counts, and citation requirements a report cannot ship without meeting. The standard I held myself to as a reviewer is now enforced structurally, on a system that produces far more analysis than I ever could by hand.
